Frequently Asked Questions

What is AKSH?
AKSH is a calm space created for people to pause, reflect, express themselves, and reconnect.
Who is AKSH for?
AKSH is for anyone who wants a safe space to talk, understand themselves, or simply explore.
What happens in a session?
A session is a calm conversation where you can share what you are experiencing and explore your thoughts.
Do I need a specific reason to join?
No. You can come even if you are just exploring or trying to understand yourself better.
How do I book a session?
You can request a session through the AKSH booking page.
